I am a PhD student at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/), based at the [Idiap Research Institute](https://www.idiap.ch/) in Martigny, Switzerland. My research focuses on speech and audio AI, representation learning, self-supervised learning, text-to-speech, voice conversion, pathological speech, spoken language models, unsupervised segmentation, and music generation.

Before the PhD, I obtained my bachelor's degree at the American University of Beirut, worked as a C/C++ software engineer at Murex, and completed my master's at EPFL, during which I interned at Logitech doing audio machine learning research.

Outside research, I enjoy playing instruments, writing songs, and producing music.
